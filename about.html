About EMCO SALES

49 years of trust, quality, and excellence in paints and cements.

Our Story

Founded in 1977, EMCO SALES has grown from a modest paint and cement retail shop into one of the region's most trusted building materials dealers. For nearly five decades, we have been the go-to destination for homeowners, contractors, and builders seeking premium quality products at fair prices.

As authorized dealers of leading brands like Asian Paints, Birla Opus, Surfa Coats, UltraTech Cement, and Priya Cement, we pride ourselves on offering only the best. Our deep product knowledge, personalized customer service, and unwavering commitment to quality have earned us a reputation that speaks for itself.

With 2 branches strategically located to serve our customers, we continue to expand our reach while maintaining the same dedication and values that our founders instilled in the business from day one.
